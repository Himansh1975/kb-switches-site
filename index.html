A FEW THINGS WORTH ASKING

Which panel is right for my room?

Start with the switches, sockets and fan controls you need, then choose a finish and size. Share your existing switchboard or room plans with the KB team for help choosing a compatible configuration.

Can I use an app or voice control?

Selected products offer app, remote and voice-control options. Requirements differ by model; ask the team to confirm Wi-Fi, accessory and compatibility requirements for your chosen panel.

Can I order through this website?

This website helps you explore the range. Contact KB by phone or WhatsApp for current pricing, availability and ordering details.

What about installation and warranty?

Ask KB for the installation instructions and warranty terms for your exact product before ordering. Electrical installation should be carried out by a qualified professional.
